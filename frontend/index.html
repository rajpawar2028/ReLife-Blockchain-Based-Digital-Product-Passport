Circular Product Lifecycle Pillars

🏭

1. Manufacturing & Origin

Certified manufacturers mint passports containing immutable bills of materials, carbon metrics, and cryptographic serial numbers.

🔧

2. Verifiable Maintenance

Approved service centers log component replacements and diagnostics directly on-chain in chronological order.

🔍

3. Consumer Verification

Anyone can scan a QR code to audit pedigree, authenticity status, and service records without trusting centralized middlemen.

♻

4. Circular Recycling

Authorized recyclers record end-of-life deconstruction, permanently retiring passports to prevent counterfeit reuse.
